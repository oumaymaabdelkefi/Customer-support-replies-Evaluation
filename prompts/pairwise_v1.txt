You are comparing two customer support replies for TechNest Gadgets, a
fictional electronics retailer. Both replies answer the same customer
message. Decide which reply is better. This is the only task: do not
answer the customer yourself and do not rewrite either reply.

## What you are given

- customer_message: what the customer asked or reported.
- policy_excerpt: the only source of truth. If it says a request isn't
  covered by policy, that is a deliberate gap: a reply that says so and
  offers to escalate is better than one that invents an answer.
- reply_A and reply_B: the two candidate replies.

## How to decide

Judge the replies on these criteria, in this order of importance:

1. Correctness: every claim about policy, timeframes, cost or process
   matches the policy_excerpt. No invented rules, order statuses,
   discounts or promises support can't keep.
2. Completeness: the customer gets every policy condition they need to
   act (timeframe, required evidence or steps, who pays, what happens
   next), or a clear request for the missing information.
3. Politeness: respectful and empathetic, no blame or sarcasm.

A reply that is wrong on correctness loses to one that is right, however
polite or detailed it is. Length is not a criterion: a longer reply is
not better unless the extra text adds correct, needed information. The
order the replies appear in is not a criterion either.

Answer "tie" only if the replies are genuinely equal on all three
criteria.

## Output format

Return exactly one JSON object and nothing else:

{{
  "winner": "<A, B, or tie>",
  "reason": "<one short sentence naming the deciding difference, cite the rule id if there is one>"
}}

## Input

customer_message:
{customer_message}

policy_excerpt:
{policy_excerpt}

reply_A:
{reply_a}

reply_B:
{reply_b}
